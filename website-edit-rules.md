# Website Edit Rules — Portfolio Adaptation

## 1. Branding
- Replace every instance of **"Cyber Solutions"** with **"SNT"** across all pages, metadata, and assets.

---

## 2. Site Structure: Multi-page → Single Page

Convert the site from multiple pages to **two pages only**:

### Page 1: Homepage (Single Scrollable Page)
Remove all separate pages (case studies, services, etc.) and consolidate into one homepage with the following sections **in order**:
1. **Hero / Intro**
2. **Services** *(previously a separate page)*
3. **Skills** *(previously a separate page or section)*
4. **Client Feedback / Testimonials** *(previously a separate page or section)*
5. **Contact** *(move the contact form/section here — no longer a standalone page)*

> ⚠️ Delete the following pages entirely:
> - Case Studies page
> - Services page
> - Any other sub-pages beyond the homepage

### Page 2: Contact Page
- Keep a dedicated `/contact` page as a standalone destination for the **"Contact Me"** button (see Rule 4).

---

## 3. Remove "Worked With" Section
- **Delete** the section titled "Worked With" (or any equivalent "clients logo / partners" strip) from the homepage entirely. Do not replace it with anything.

---

## 4. Navigation Bar
- Remove links to pages that no longer exist (case studies, services, etc.) from the nav bar.
- Remove the **Contact** link from the navigation bar — contact is now accessed via the button (see Rule 5).
- Keep navigation minimal, reflecting the new single-page structure.

---

## 5. Newsletter Button → "Contact Me" Button
- Replace the **newsletter signup button** with a **"Contact Me"** button.
- The button must:
  - Be visually distinct — use a **different color** from the rest of the UI (e.g., an accent color not used elsewhere in the nav or hero).
  - Be a **standalone button element**, not a nav bar item or inline text link.
  - Link to the dedicated `/contact` page.

---

## 6. Contact Section on Homepage
- At the bottom of the homepage, embed a **contact section** (e.g., a short form or contact details).
- This is separate from the `/contact` page — it serves as a quick in-page option for visitors who scroll through the homepage.
