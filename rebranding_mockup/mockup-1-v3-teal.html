Questions

The ones that come up on nearly every first call.

How long does a project take?[−]

A website is usually 3–4 weeks. A full Notion system is 2–3 weeks, depending on how many people touch it. Automations take 1–2 weeks each once we can reach your data.

Do we need all three services?[+]

No. Most clients start with the single thing that hurts most. Because it's one studio, the second piece plugs into the first instead of becoming another silo.

What happens after launch?[+]

You own everything — the site, the workspace, the automations. We train your team, write the documentation, and stay on call for a support window.

Will this replace my staff?[+]

That's not what we build. Automation takes the repetitive middle of a job. Judgement, relationships and the awkward edge cases stay with your people — that's where their value is.

What does it cost?[+]

Most projects land between $800 and $2,500 depending on scope. You get a fixed number before we start — no hourly billing, no surprises.
